In my research project **TraceMind**, I investigated automated Root Cause Analysis (RCA) in cascading microservice failures. I designed a graph-constrained causal reasoning engine that builds dynamic Service Dependency Graphs (SDGs) from OpenTelemetry traces and fuses multi-modal metric Z-scores, span error rates, and crash signals. By constraining evidence attribution to reverse topological reachability paths, TraceMind prevents downstream symptom nodes from outranking true root-cause originators. Evaluated on **CausalOpsBench**, TraceMind achieved 100.0% Top-1 RCA Accuracy and 1.00 MRR with sub-millisecond execution latency, outperforming metric IsolationForest (83.33%) and unconstrained LLMs (0.0%).
