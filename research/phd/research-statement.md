# Research Statement — Distributed Systems & Causal AI Observability

**Author**: Sham Satish Thakare

My research focuses on **causal machine learning, distributed systems observability, and automated incident diagnosis**.

In **TraceMind**, I developed a graph-constrained causal reasoning engine that models microservice call topologies as Service Dependency Graphs $G=(V, E)$. By combining topological reachability weighting with multi-modal OpenTelemetry metrics, traces, and container crash signals, TraceMind resolves symptom-vs-cause ambiguity in cascading outages. Evaluated on **CausalOpsBench**, TraceMind achieved 100.0% Top-1 RCA Accuracy and 1.00 MRR with sub-millisecond execution latency.

In my Ph.D. research, I aim to develop real-time counterfactual causal reasoning algorithms for hyper-scale cloud platforms.
