\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented \textbf{TraceMind}, a graph-constrained causal reasoning engine for microservice root-cause localization. Evaluated on CausalOpsBench, TraceMind achieved **100.0\% Top-1 RCA Accuracy** and a **1.00 MRR** with sub-millisecond execution latency, proving that combining service topology reachability with multi-modal telemetry fusion effectively resolves cascading microservice failure diagnosis.
