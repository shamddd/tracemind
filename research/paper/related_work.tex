\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\paragraph{Microservice RCA Systems}
MicroRCA \cite{wu2020microrca} and CauseInfer \cite{chen2014causeinfer} introduced service dependency graphs for latency attribution. CloudRCA \cite{meng2020cloudrca} applied supervised graph neural networks for enterprise incident localization.

\paragraph{Statistical & Log Anomaly Detection}
IsolationForest \cite{liu2008isolation} is widely used for high-dimensional metric anomaly detection. DeepLog \cite{du2017deeplog} models log key sequences using LSTMs. TraceMind extends this body of work by integrating multi-modal telemetry metrics, traces, and crash signals onto topological dependency graphs.
