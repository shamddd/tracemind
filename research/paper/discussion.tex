\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

Our findings underscore the necessity of structural topology constraints in automated incident diagnosis. Unconstrained LLM reasoning agents frequently mistake downstream HTTP 504 Gateway Timeouts for the origin failure, because gateway logs accumulate the largest volume of error messages.

TraceMind demonstrates that combining topological reachability with multi-modal telemetry feature drift eliminates symptom hallucinations and delivers deterministic root-cause localization.
