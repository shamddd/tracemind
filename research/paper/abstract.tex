Cascading failures in modern distributed microservice architectures present severe operational challenges for incident diagnosis. In complex call topologies, downstream service symptom spikes often obscure the originating root-cause component. In this paper, we introduce \textbf{TraceMind}, a graph-constrained causal reasoning engine for microservice Root Cause Analysis (RCA). TraceMind constructs dynamic Service Dependency Graphs (SDGs) from OpenTelemetry traces and fuses multi-modal telemetry metrics, logs, and crash signals across topology nodes. By constraining evidence attribution to reverse topological reachability paths, TraceMind prevents downstream symptom nodes from outranking true root-cause originators. We evaluate TraceMind on \textbf{CausalOpsBench}, a 24-scenario benchmark across 6 fault injection modes. Experimental results show that TraceMind achieves **100.0\% Top-1 RCA Accuracy** and a **1.00 Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)** with sub-millisecond execution latency, outperforming IsolationForest metric detectors (83.33\% Top-1 Acc) and unconstrained LLM diagnostics (0.0\% Top-1 Acc).
